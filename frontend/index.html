OUR COMMITMENT & VALUES

A tradition of academic rigor rooted in ethical responsibility and innovation.

Established to foster intellectual leadership in the heart of Addis Ababa, Abyssinia Academy cultivates academic distinction, ethical integrity, and civic commitment across Grades 9 through 12.

Our curriculum unites rigorous STEM education, trilingual fluency (Amharic, English, and French), and active scientific research with African and global humanities. In fully equipped modern science suites and collaborative learning commons, our students master complex problem-solving while building lifelong moral clarity and dedication to the wider community.

"Integrity, Respect, and Intellectual Honesty"

Every student at Abyssinia Academy commits to our standard of ethical scholarship. We champion independent thinking, respect for diverse viewpoints, and personal responsibility in every classroom and examination hall.

  • • Commitment to honest original academic research and reporting
  • • Student-led Prefect and Honor Council advocating mutual respect
  • • Zero tolerance for academic dishonesty or harassment
verified_userIntegrity Covenant

A tradition of mutual trust and character building in every cohort.

Eight Historic Residential Houses

Upon entry, scholars are sorted into one of eight historic boarding houses—Dunstan, Cranmer, More, Wolsey, Latimer, Ridley, Bede, or Anselm. Daily house meetings, formal family-style dinners, and the annual House Shield debate foster lifelong fraternal bonds.

  • • Resident Housemasters and Junior Resident Tutors in every hall
  • • Weekly Inter-House Academic Quads and Choral Contests
  • • Vertical integration: 9th through 12th graders share commons spaces
cottage Residential Community

82% of student body lives on campus with 100% faculty co-residency.

Ethics in Action Beyond the Campus Wall

Every Form V scholar undertakes 120 verified hours of civic engagement or ecological conservancy along the Millbrook Estuary. Our global fellows spend spring trimesters conducting agricultural field research in Oaxaca or heritage conservation in Oxford.

  • • The Millbrook Marine Ecology Watershed Sanctuary
  • • Fully endowed international summer exchange grants
  • • Weekly student-run literacy tutoring in Boston public libraries
public Civic Responsibility

Over 24,000 community service hours pledged annually.
